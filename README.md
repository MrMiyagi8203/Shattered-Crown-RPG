# Dungeons of the Shattered Crown — Ascendant Edition
## New Player Guide

Welcome to **Dungeons of the Shattered Crown!** The Crown has shattered, its seal is failing, and the Hollow Tide threatens the realm. Your journey is to explore the world, grow stronger, collect the three Crown Shards, and face the final challenge waiting in the Sunken Vault.

## 1. Getting Started

1. Open `shattered-crown-ascendant.html` in a modern web browser.
2. Start a new game and create your character.
3. Choose a class that fits your preferred play style.
4. Begin exploring and learn the game's mechanics as you progress.

Your progress is saved in your browser. Avoid clearing your browser data if you want to keep your save file.

## 2. Character Classes

Choose from six classes:

- **Fighter:** A durable melee specialist with extra health.
- **Rogue:** An agile precision fighter who starts with twin daggers.
- **Wizard:** An arcane spellcaster who starts with an Ashwood Staff.
- **Cleric:** A divine warrior and healer who starts in Blessed Mail.
- **Ranger:** A wilderness hunter who starts with a Hunter's Bow.
- **Paladin:** An oath-sworn knight with extra health and Oathplate.

Each class offers a different play style, so choose the one you find most interesting.

## 3. Your Main Objective

Your adventure follows a simple progression:

1. Explore the world and discover new encounters.
2. Fight enemies, complete quests, and collect rewards.
3. Return to Cinderwatch to rest, shop, and improve your equipment.
4. Become stronger by leveling up and unlocking skills.
5. Defeat the boss lords and collect all three Crown Shards.
6. Travel to the Sunken Vault and face Kaelen.

Your choices and preparation will help determine how well you survive the journey.

## 4. Combat Basics

Combat uses dice rolls and character statistics.

- **Weapon attacks:** Roll a d20 plus modifiers against the enemy's Armor Class (AC).
- **Skills:** Equip up to two skills before battle. Each equipped skill can be used once per battle.
- **Potions:** Restore health when you need them.
- **Talk or Persuade:** Attempt to convince an enemy to surrender instead of fighting to the death.
- **Flee:** Attempt to escape a dangerous encounter.
- **Inspect Enemy:** Learn about an enemy's traits and rumored weaknesses.
- **Auto-Battle:** Let your character fight automatically, use potions and skills, and potentially retreat when health is low.

Persuasion requires four progress against regular enemies and six against bosses. Successful persuasion adds two progress, while failure adds one. Appealing to mercy or honor, or identifying an enemy's weakness, may help.

Fleeing uses a difficulty check: DC 11 against regular enemies and DC 17 against bosses.

**Beginner tip:** Watch your health and use your abilities carefully. Bosses are more difficult than ordinary enemies, so prepare before challenging them.

## 5. Equipment and Loot

Enemies can drop weapons, armor, and other rewards.

Equipment rarities range from **Common to Legendary**.

- Equip better weapons and armor as you find them.
- Matching equipment set pieces provide bonuses at two and three pieces.
- Visit the merchant in Cinderwatch to purchase supplies.
- Use the forge and upgrade equipment to improve your gear.
- Sell or scrap equipment you no longer need.

Remember to keep materials for future upgrades instead of spending or selling everything immediately.

## 6. Scavenging and Materials

Scavenging helps you gather resources for equipment upgrades.

- Start gathering when you have access to a gathering node.
- Improve your scavenging level to increase gathering speed and your chance of finding extra materials.
- Gathering pauses during combat.
- Your camp can continue gathering while you are away, at half speed for up to eight hours.

Check your collected materials regularly so you can keep improving your equipment.

## 7. Cinderwatch

Cinderwatch is your main place to prepare between adventures.

Here you can:

- Rest to restore your health.
- Visit the merchant to purchase equipment and supplies.
- Use the forge to work with materials.
- Visit the Ember Tavern.
- Check the quest board.
- Access other town services and menus.

Make a habit of returning to town before tackling difficult encounters.

## 8. Important Menus

- **Adventure:** Explore the world and continue your journey.
- **Bag:** Manage equipment, items, selling, and scrapping.
- **Skills:** Review unlocked abilities and equip your skills.
- **Character:** View your character information and statistics.
- **Travel:** Access available travel options.
- **Quests:** Review objectives and rewards.
- **Hall of Deeds:** Track achievements and claim available gold rewards.
- **Tavern Dice:** Play a dice game against the house.
- **Settings:** Adjust display and gameplay options, and export or import saved data.

## 9. Keyboard Shortcuts

You can use the on-screen buttons or these keyboard shortcuts.

| Key | Action |

| H | Adventure |
| B | Bag / Inventory |
| K | Skills |
| C | Character |
| T | Travel |
| Q | Quests |
| A | Hall of Deeds |
| D | Tavern Dice |
| 1–7 | Select a combat action when available |
| Esc | Go back or leave the current screen |

Keyboard shortcuts are optional. You can play using the on-screen controls.

## 10. Saving Your Progress

Your game saves in your browser.

To protect your progress:

1. Open Settings.
2. Find the Save Data section.
3. Select **Export to Box** to display your save data and attempt to copy it.
4. Keep a backup somewhere safe.
5. To restore a backup, paste valid save data into the box and select **Import from Box**.
6. Return to the title screen and select Continue.

Do not clear your browser's stored data without backing up your save first.

## 11. Beginner Tips

- Rest in Cinderwatch when your health is low.
- Save a backup before attempting difficult content.
- Equip your two most useful skills before challenging a boss.
- Inspect enemies to learn about their abilities and weaknesses.
- Keep healing potions available for emergencies.
- Complete quests to earn additional rewards.
- Gather materials to strengthen your equipment.
- Check your inventory regularly and replace outdated gear.
- Use persuasion if you want to attempt a peaceful resolution.
- Prepare before traveling into dangerous regions.

## Final Words

The Crown is broken, the seal is failing, and the realm needs a hero.

Explore carefully, grow stronger, discover the secrets of the world, and decide the fate of the Shattered Crown.

Good luck, adventurer!
